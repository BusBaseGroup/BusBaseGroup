Lamsey Lane diversion announcement
=================================

Applies to routes 34, 35 and 36.

Passenger warning:
“This bus is on diversion. Please speak to the driver.”

Trigger points while the passenger display is showing THIS STOP:
- Inbound towards King’s Lynn: Heacham Bottom Farm and Poplar Ave / Poplar Avenue.
- Outbound away from King’s Lynn: Heacham Bottom Farm only.

For route 36, outbound includes journeys showing Hunstanton, Wells-next-the-Sea or Fakenham as the destination.
