BusBase Every Stop AI fix
=========================

The previous build only had full recordings for the admin timing-point list.
This build changes the live Next Stop screen so EVERY live stop is passed into
the shared admin AI player.

Playback order:
1. Try a stored full clear-AI sentence if one exists.
2. Otherwise play a fresh clear-AI "Next stop" / "This stop" prefix.
3. Then play the dedicated clear-AI recording for the exact physical stop name.

No browser/system text-to-speech is used.

Routes 34, 35 and 36 have been expanded from timing points to their current
physical stop-name bank, including intermediate stops such as Kettlewell Lane,
Highgate School, Health Centre, Elvington Road, Pansey Drive, Station Road,
Strickland Avenue, Bottom Farm, Collingwood Close, Fenside, Fengate,
Jennings Close, College Drive, Poplar Avenue, Nourse Drive, Redgate Hill,
Jolly Sailors, Dalegate Market, Church Walk and the other current Coastliner
physical stop labels stored in the bank.

Lamsey Lane: routes 34/35/36 show and play "Diversion in operation." while
travelling Bottom Farm <-> Poplar Avenue.
Whiteway Road: the passing-place passenger information remains enabled.
